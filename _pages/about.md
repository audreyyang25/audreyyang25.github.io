I am a senior at Harvard College passionate about computer science, philosophy, and music. My primary research interests are training domain-specific AI models and designing AI applications to facilitate information accessibility, particularly in law, education, and finance. I have been a research intern at Harvard Law School and Harvard Graduate School of Education, and I am currently a research intern at MIT Sloan, working on a project to design a reliable AI financial advisor using knowledge-base grounding methods. Some of my current research curiosities center around how to design benchmarks for tasks that lack ground truths, and how we should spread AI literacy to train healthy epistemic habits when interacting with LLMs.

In my free time, I enjoy discussing metaethics and thinking about the nature of objective morality, how ethical subjectivity is reflected in current LLM biases, and what exactly we are aligning LLMs to when it comes to morality. I am also a musician, originally a classical pianist and now a musical theater keys player and music director. In my spare time, I enjoy volunteering at Boston Josiah Quincy Elementary School, reading fantasy and sci-fi novels, baking eclectic goods, and going on hikes!
