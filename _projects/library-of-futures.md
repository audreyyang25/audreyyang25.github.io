_Library of Futures_ explores how 100 years of science-fiction film have predicted, warned, and dreamed about our futures. Built with Python, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, and D3 for Harvard's CS 1710 (Visualization) in Fall 2025, where it was awarded **Best Project**. Made with Michael Cai, Tanvir Haque, and Sydney Nguyen.
